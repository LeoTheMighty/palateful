Comparing the Three Directions A — Editorial Cookbook: Hero card for tonight's meal, horizontal scroll for sections. Feels like NYT Cooking — editorial, spacious, content-first. Best for the "zero-scroll home" vision.

B — Card Grid: Pinterest-style 2-column grid with tab navigation. More recipes visible at once. FAB for adding. Best if browsing/discovery is more important than contextual prediction.

C — List-Centric: Apple Notes-inspired list with thumbnails. Information-dense but still warm. Book tags give context. Best for quick scanning when you know what you're looking for.

GF Lemon Scones

Forked from: Classic English Scones · Baking Projects
⏱ 45 min 🍽 8 scones 📖 My Recipes
v5 · Last edited 3 days ago · View history
Baking Gluten-Free Breakfast

Ingredients

GF flour blend2 cups
Unsalted butter, cold½ cup
Sugar¼ cup
Lemon zest2 tbsp
Buttermilk¾ cup
Baking powder1 tbsp
Salt½ tsp
▶ Start Cooking
🛒 Add to Cart
📅 Plan Meal
↗ Share

Steps

1

Preheat oven to 400°F. Line baking sheet with parchment paper.

2

Whisk GF flour, sugar, baking powder, lemon zest, and salt in a large bowl.

3

Cut cold butter into flour mixture until pea-sized crumbs form. Add 2 tbsp milk to dough.

📝 Notes (v5)

Higher hydration doesn't work with this flour brand. Stick with ¾ cup buttermilk. Added extra milk in step 3 for v5.

Cooking Mode Comparison A — Dark: Chocolate background with cream text. Higher contrast for kitchen lighting. Reduces screen glare on wet/shiny countertops. Step text at 24px, timer at prominent size. Feels like a distinct "kitchen tool" mode — unmistakable shift from curation.

B — Light: Stays in the warm cream palette. Consistent with the rest of the app. Still large type and oversized targets, but no jarring mode shift. Feels more like the same cookbook, just zoomed in.

Shared elements: Ingredient strip (scrollable), step dots for progress, voice button (center), large prev/next buttons, active timer bar, recipe name in header. All touch targets are 48dp+.
