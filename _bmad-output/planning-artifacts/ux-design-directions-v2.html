Hybrid Home — Design Notes The hero card appears only when a meal is planned for today — it's contextual, not permanent. Below it, a 2-column card grid shows recent recipes with Playfair serif titles and photo-dominant cards. Search bar is always visible at top. Section headers use Playfair with "See all" links. Collection count anchors the bottom. The "Cook" shortcut on the hero card is one tap to cooking mode — zero intermediate screens.

GF Lemon Scones

Forked from: Classic English Scones · Baking Projects
⏱ 45 min 🍽 8 scones 📖 My Recipes
v5 · Last edited 3 days ago · View history
Baking Gluten-Free Breakfast

Ingredients

GF flour blend2 cups
Unsalted butter, cold½ cup
Sugar¼ cup
Lemon zest2 tbsp
Buttermilk¾ cup
Baking powder1 tbsp
Salt½ tsp
▶ Start Cooking
🛒 Add to Cart
📅 Plan Meal
↗ Share

Steps

1

Preheat oven to 400°F. Line baking sheet with parchment paper.

2

Whisk GF flour, sugar, baking powder, lemon zest, and salt in a large bowl.

3

Cut cold butter into flour mixture until pea-sized crumbs form. Add 2 tbsp milk to dough.

📝 Notes (v5)

Higher hydration doesn't work with this flour brand. Stick with ¾ cup buttermilk. Added extra milk in step 3 for v5.

Cooking Mode — Cream on Chocolate

Font Color Comparison Both use chocolate (#4A3728) background — warm, not black. The difference is subtle but meaningful:
Element A — Standard Cream B — Warm Ivory
Step text #FAF7F2 (cream) #F5ECD7 (warm ivory)
Contrast ratio ~8.5:1 (AAA) ~7.2:1 (AAA)
Feel Clean, slightly cool against chocolate Warmer, like aged paper — more "cookbook"
Accents Terracotta unchanged Terracotta unchanged
Recommendation Both pass WCAG AAA. The warm ivory (#F5ECD7) is subtly warmer and feels more cohesive with the terracotta accents and hazelnut secondary text. It's the "aged cookbook page" vs. "fresh paper" distinction. Very subtle — either works beautifully.
