Potato Quiche

A hearty and delicious quiche loaded with potatoes, cheese, and herbs.

Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 45 minutes
Servings: 6

Ingredients:
1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
3 large eggs
1 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup whole milk
2 cups diced potatoes, cooked
1 cup shredded Gruyere cheese
1/2 cup diced onion, sauteed
2 tablespoons fresh chives, chopped
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/4 teaspoon black pepper
1/4 teaspoon nutmeg

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F.
2. Place the pie crust in a 9-inch pie dish and crimp the edges.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, heavy cream, and milk until smooth.
4. Spread the cooked diced potatoes evenly over the bottom of the pie crust.
5. Sprinkle the Gruyere cheese and sauteed onion over the potatoes.
6. Pour the egg mixture over the filling.
7. Sprinkle the chives, salt, pepper, and nutmeg on top.
8. Bake for 40-45 minutes until the center is set and the top is golden brown.
9. Let the quiche cool for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
