Lemon Blueberry Pancakes

Fluffy lemon-scented pancakes studded with fresh blueberries. Brunch, sorted.

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
2 cups all-purpose flour
2 tablespoons sugar
2 teaspoons baking powder
1/2 teaspoon salt
2 large eggs
1 3/4 cups buttermilk
1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
1 teaspoon lemon zest
1 cup fresh blueberries

Directions:
1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In another bowl, whisk eggs, buttermilk, melted butter, and lemon zest.
3. Fold wet into dry; batter should be lumpy.
4. Gently fold in blueberries.
5. Heat a griddle over medium heat; cook pancakes until bubbles form, then flip.

Cinnamon Sugar French Toast

Custardy French toast with a crackly cinnamon sugar finish.

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Serves: 2

Ingredients:
4 slices thick-cut brioche
2 large eggs
1/2 cup whole milk
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
2 tablespoons unsalted butter
1/4 cup granulated sugar
1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Directions:
1. Whisk eggs, milk, and vanilla in a shallow dish.
2. Combine sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl.
3. Dip each slice of brioche in the custard, turning once.
4. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat; cook bread 2-3 minutes per side.
5. Roll hot French toast in cinnamon sugar before serving.
