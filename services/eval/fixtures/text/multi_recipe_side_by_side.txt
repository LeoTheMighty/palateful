Garlic Herb Butter
A compound butter for steaks, bread, or roasted vegetables.

Prep: 5 min. Makes 1 stick.

1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped
1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
1/4 teaspoon black pepper

1. Mash butter with a fork until smooth.
2. Stir in garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper.
3. Scrape onto plastic wrap, roll into a log, and chill until firm.

Chimichurri

Bright green Argentine sauce. Eat with grilled meats.

Prep: 10 min. Makes 1 cup.

1 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
3 cloves garlic, minced
2 tablespoons fresh oregano, chopped
1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

1. Combine parsley, garlic, and oregano in a bowl.
2. Whisk in olive oil, red wine vinegar, pepper flakes, and salt.
3. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.
