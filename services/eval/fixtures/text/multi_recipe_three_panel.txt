Tomato Salsa

A chunky salsa that gets better after an hour of chilling.

Serves: 6

4 ripe tomatoes, diced
1/2 red onion, finely diced
1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
2 tablespoons lime juice
1 teaspoon kosher salt

Combine everything. Taste. Add salt if needed. Chill 1 hour.

Variation: swap the tomatoes for mango for a sweeter sauce.

Guacamole

Creamy, spicy, smashy.

Serves: 4

3 ripe avocados, halved and pitted
1 lime, juiced
1/4 cup red onion, finely diced
1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
2 tablespoons cilantro, chopped
1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

Scoop avocados into a bowl. Add lime juice and mash to desired texture. Stir in onion, jalapeño, cilantro, and salt.

Substitution Notes: add diced tomato or crumbled cotija for richer guac.

Pico de Gallo

Bright, raw, and just-right sharp.

Serves: 4

3 plum tomatoes, finely diced
1/4 cup white onion, finely diced
1 jalapeño, minced
2 tablespoons cilantro, chopped
2 tablespoons lime juice
1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

Combine all ingredients in a bowl. Let rest 15 minutes before serving.
