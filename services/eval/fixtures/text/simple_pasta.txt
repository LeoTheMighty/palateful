Simple Garlic Butter Pasta

A quick weeknight dinner that's ready in 20 minutes. Just a handful of pantry staples and you're good to go.

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Serves: 2

Ingredients:
8 ounces spaghetti
3 tablespoons unsalted butter
4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese
Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes and cook for 1-2 minutes until the garlic is golden and fragrant. Be careful not to burn it.
3. Add the drained pasta to the skillet and toss to coat in the garlic butter. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water to loosen the sauce if needed.
4. Remove from heat, toss with Parmesan cheese, and season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately.
