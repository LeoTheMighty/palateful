Privacy Policy

Effective 18 April 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

Palateful is a kitchen-management mobile app (iOS and Android) and companion web experience that helps you capture, organize, and cook your recipes. This policy explains what data the Palateful app and backend services collect, who we share it with, and how long we keep it. If anything here is unclear, or you want a copy of your data or a deletion, email us at leonid@ac93.org.

2. Data we collect

Account data

When you sign in we store your email address, your display name, and a stable account identifier issued by our auth provider (Auth0). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we additionally receive the subset of profile fields those providers return (typically email, name, and profile picture URL). We do not receive or store your password — Auth0 handles authentication.

Recipe content you create

Recipes, ingredients, steps, notes, photos, audio memos, and video memos you add to the app are stored in our database and (for media) in Amazon S3 buckets we control. This content is yours — we only use it to provide the app's core features (syncing across your devices, searching your library, cooking mode playback).

Shared household data

If you invite someone to a shared recipe book, shopping list, or meal-plan calendar, the contents of those resources are visible to the members you invite. Membership is explicit: nothing is shared publicly or discovered by other users without an invitation.

Device and crash data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to capture crash reports. A crash report includes your device model, operating system version, a stack trace, and an anonymous install identifier generated by Firebase. Crash reports do not include your recipes, photos, or account email.

Notification data

If you opt into push notifications, we register your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging (on Android) and Apple Push Notification Service (on iOS). We store the resulting push token alongside your account so we can deliver notifications you asked for (import finished, household activity, cooking timers). You can turn these off at any time from the operating system's notification settings.

Diagnostic and performance data

Palateful collects anonymous performance telemetry so we can detect app slowdowns and crashes before they affect you. The data is aggregate, not per-person, and never includes recipe content, photos, messages, or other personal data. Specifically we measure:

  • Cold-start time — how long the app takes to reach the first frame after launch.
  • Screen paint time — how long each screen takes to render after you tap its tab or link. Screen identifiers are redacted to templates (e.g. /recipes/:id/edit rather than the literal recipe ID).
  • Network-request latency — how long each HTTP request to our backend takes, with the URL likewise redacted to its template form.
  • Frame jank — a per-minute aggregate of frames that rendered slower than the target refresh rate. No frame content is sampled.
  • iOS MetricKit daily aggregates — the iOS operating system reports daily roll-ups of launch time, responsiveness, CPU time, cellular upload, disk writes, and memory usage. Apple generates these on-device; we forward the aggregate, not the individual events.
  • Android JankStats per-minute aggregates — the Android operating system reports the number of janky frames per minute. We forward the count, not the rendered content.

The same data is also sampled by Google Firebase Performance Monitoring as a secondary cross-check. Firebase receives the URL + status code of HTTP requests (with the same redaction) and the app-start timing. Diagnostic data is kept for 30 days and then purged automatically.

AI chat messages (opt-in)

If you use the in-app AI assistant, the prompts you send and the responses you receive are forwarded to our language-model subprocessors (OpenAI and/or Anthropic) to generate the reply. The assistant is strictly opt-in and clearly labelled in the UI. We do not persist AI chat content on our servers beyond the request lifecycle, except that we may retain a small random sample for up to 90 days to review reply quality and debug incidents.

3. Third-party subprocessors

Palateful relies on the following subprocessors. Each is named with the data shared and the purpose it serves:

  • Auth0 (Okta, Inc.) — authentication. Receives your email address, display name, and provider account identifier so we can recognize you on return visits.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — server infrastructure and media storage. Hosts our application servers, Postgres database, and the S3 buckets that store your recipe photos, audio memos, and video memos.
  • Google Firebase — crash reporting (Crashlytics), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and performance monitoring (Firebase Performance). Receives device and OS metadata, your push token, and anonymous app-start + HTTP latency samples; never receives your recipe content, photos, or account email.
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. — AI assistant replies (opt-in only). Receives the chat messages you send the assistant and the relevant recipe context required to answer your prompt. Governed by OpenAI's API Data Usage policy; we do not opt into training-data contribution.
  • Anthropic, PBC — AI assistant replies (opt-in only, same feature). Same data shared as OpenAI, routed to Anthropic's Claude model when selected as the backend. Governed by Anthropic's API Terms; we do not opt into training-data contribution.
  • Google Play (Android) — store distribution and, in the future, subscription billing. Receives only the data Google Play collects as part of its store and billing APIs (device identifier, purchase tokens). No subscription product is live today; this is reserved for future releases.
  • Apple App Store (iOS) — store distribution and, in the future, subscription billing via In-App Purchase. Same scope as Google Play but for iOS devices.
  • Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) — push delivery on iOS devices. Receives your device push token and the notification payloads we originate.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with advertising networks. Palateful runs no third-party advertising SDKs.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Account data and recipe content — kept while your account is active. On deletion request, we remove your account and associated content within 30 days (see Deletion, below).
  • Crash logs — retained for up to 90 days by Firebase Crashlytics, then aged out.
  • Push tokens — retained while your device is opted into notifications; removed when you disable notifications or delete your account.
  • AI chat messages — not persisted server-side beyond the request lifecycle, except for a small random sample kept up to 90 days for quality review.

5. Children

Palateful is designated Teen (13+) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, we do not make a COPPA compliance claim, and we do not run targeted advertising of any kind — ever. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, the following rights may apply. We honor them for every Palateful user regardless of jurisdiction:

  • GDPR (EU / UK) — right of access, rectification, erasure, portability, and to object to processing.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California) — right to know what we collect, right to delete, right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, email leonid@ac93.org. We will respond within 30 days.

7. How to request deletion

Send an email to leonid@ac93.org from the address on your Palateful account. We will confirm receipt, verify that the request came from you, and permanently delete your account and content within 30 days. Backups containing your data age out of our systems within an additional 30 days, after which no copy remains.

8. Data transfers

Palateful's servers run in the United States. If you access the app from outside the United States, you consent to your data being processed in the United States. Our subprocessors may operate in additional regions; we rely on the standard contractual clauses they offer where required.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our app, our subprocessors, or the law. Material changes will be announced in the app and/or via email to the address on your account. The "Effective" date at the top of this page and the version number below always reflect the current version.

10. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or privacy complaints: leonid@ac93.org.
