Why we're free

Palateful is founder-funded. There's no investor in the room asking how we'll monetize you, no quarterly target that turns "import a recipe" into a feature behind a paywall. The cheapest way to keep your kitchen data yours is to never build the machinery that holds it hostage.

This commitment is locked into the codebase: a CI check fails any pull request that introduces words like "premium," "subscription," or "upgrade." Free forever — and the build proves it.
